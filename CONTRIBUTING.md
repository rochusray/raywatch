# Contributing to Raywatch
Thanks for helping in development of the Raywatch.

## Who can contribute?
Anyone is welcome to contribute in any form they like, not just developers.

## Who can't contribute?
- AI agents. We do not want any contributions, from AIs. ([_our AI policy_](CONTRIBUTING.md#ai-policy))
- Anyone who is incompatible with [our values](README.md#our-values).

## Contribute by sponsoring
This project is currently paid out of Rochus Ray's own pocket. If you want to help cover the costs for the development of Raywatch, you can contribute [here](http://buymeacoffee.com/rochusray).

# AI Policy
## You may:
- ✔️ Use LLMs to write git commit descriptions.
- ✔️ Use LLMs to help find bugs.
- ✔️ Use LLMs for research purposes / looking up things.
## You may not:
- ❌ Use AI in your contributions.
- ❌ Train AI on data from this repository.
