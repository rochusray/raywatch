# Raywatch
**An open-source, privacy-first smart(_er_)watch designed to be an all day, every day companion to help you navigate your way trugh life.**
> **Project status:** Early-stage development.

# Why Raywatch?
Most smartwatches are designed as an extension to your phone. Raywatch is build different.
## Core principles
- **Longevity:** Raywatch is meant to be a robust, long living, repairable, watertight, reliable device.
- **Independence:** Raywatch aims to be completely independent from the power grid, as well as the internet or other centralized networks.
- **Security & Privacy:** A core requirement is that Raywatch must be a secure device at all times. It will feature a [TPM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trusted_Platform_Module), used for providing a key vault with integrated anti tampering protections, as well as encryption and decryption of sensible data and messages. Raywatch is meant to be a cryptographic fortress, build to resist. Also, Raywatch will never store or share any data without direct consent.
- **Open Source:** The Raywatch project is open source under an ethical license. Therefore it is - and will always be - open for anyone to contribute, modify or check the software and hardware.
- **Affordability:** Raywatch is aimed to be as affordable as possible. The current plan is to price it around 250€.

## Applications
- **Resistance:** Raywatch is build to not only resist against cyber attacks, but also to aid in resistance against oppression, repression, regimes and cooperations.
- **Physical Health & Disabilities:** Raywatch has multiple builtin sensors to be able to collect and analyze data about your physical health, while also never sharing it with your health insurance. It shall also provide assistance for people with disabilities.
- **Mental Health & Neurodivergents:** Raywatch started out with the purpose of tracking mental health symptoms, to detect patterns and assist therapy. This is still a prime functionality, as well as providing additional features to aid people in their lives with physical illnesses and neurodivergents.
- **Communication:** In groups, Raywatches are able to communicate via the [Meshtastic](https://meshtastic.org/) network, but separately encrypted. For maximum security it will be possible to do a local key exchange via NFC.

## Hardware Features
Here is a list of hardware features Raywatch is planned to have:
- TPM
- MicroSD card slot
- Multiple high accuracy accelerometers for acceleration and position tracking
- 3-Axis magnetometer for orientation sensing
- Heart rate & O₂ blood level sensor
- 10 buttons
- Transflective RGB LCD display
- ~60 RGB LEDs 
- BLE (_Bluetooth Low Energy_) to connect to your phone
- Meshtastic & LoRa for independend communication
- Vibration motor for discrete notifications
- 200mAh replacable battery
- NFC
- Wireless charging
- Solar powered charging
